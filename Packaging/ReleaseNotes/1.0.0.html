Lampo 1.0

The first public release: Apple's on-device model on your selection, your screen and your clipboard.

  • Panel (⌥⇧Space): ask from any app, with your selection, clipboard or window as context.
  • Correct, Rewrite, Translate in place, in any app, with ⌘Z to undo.
  • Reply (⌥⇧M) in the message's language; Dictate (⌥⇧D) on device, cleaned up and typed at the cursor.
  • Ask the screen (⌥⇧S), Create event / reminder (⌥⇧E / ⌥⇧P) and Extract (⌥⇧X) contacts, tables and to-dos.
  • Your own actions, a step-by-step welcome guide, English and Italian.

Everything runs on your Mac unless you ask for Private Cloud Compute.
