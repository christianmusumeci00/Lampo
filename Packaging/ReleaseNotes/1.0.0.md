The first public release of Lampo: Apple's on-device model on your selection, your screen and your clipboard, from the menu bar.

**Requires macOS 27 on Apple silicon, with Apple Intelligence turned on.**

### What's inside
- **Panel** (⌥⇧Space): ask from any app; attach your selection, clipboard or the front window; copy, insert, follow up, go deeper.
- **Correct** (⌥⇧C), **Rewrite** (⌥⇧R, four tones) and **Translate** (⌥⇧T, Apple Translation) in place, in any app, with ⌘Z to undo.
- **Reply** (⌥⇧M): a draft in the message's language, refined in yours.
- **Dictate** (⌥⇧D): on-device speech, hesitations removed, typed at the cursor.
- **Ask the screen** (⌥⇧S): questions about the window you're using, with its text read on the Mac.
- **Create event / reminder** (⌥⇧E / ⌥⇧P) and **Extract** (⌥⇧X: contact, table, to-dos), always behind a confirmation card.
- Custom actions with your own prompts and shortcuts, Shortcuts app actions and `lampo://ask?q=` links.
- A step-by-step welcome guide; English and Italian.

### Install
Download `Lampo-1.0.0.zip` below, unzip it and move **Lampo.app** to Applications. Or with Homebrew:

```
brew install --cask christianmusumeci00/tap/lampo
```

The app is signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple. Updates arrive automatically (Sparkle).

### Privacy
Requests run on Apple's model on your Mac. Private Cloud Compute is used only when you ask, and a badge always shows where an answer ran. No telemetry, no account.
